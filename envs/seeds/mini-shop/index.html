    How a purchase flows
    Buy → demo-shop → shop-order-api, which reads the product from shop-catalog-api, takes stock from shop-inventory-api (live stock lives in the redis container), then saves the order in SQLite.
    Orbit started all five in dependency order and injected every service URL. Try orbit down shop-inventory-api, buy again, then bring it back with orbit up shop-inventory-api.
